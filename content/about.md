This is where I write about the tech I work on and mess around with: AI skill write-ups,
gamedev, and whatever else I've got going at the time. It's all technical, and most of it is
stuff I found fun enough to want to explain.

## How it gets written

I'm a rambling writer. For the longest time I wanted to write about this stuff, but
procrastination kept winning. The real blocker was the writing process itself. I'd find a
topic, start planning out the structure and the examples, and then realize I'd talked myself
into a four-page essay on something super niche that nobody would care about (or so I told
myself). So I'd drop it. And usually by the time I had it all planned out, I'd already moved on
to the next feature, so the whole cycle would just start over.

Two things changed. First, I decided I don't care, I'm writing it anyway. Second, AI got good
enough to take my ramblings and mostly work out what I meant. So now I just write, hand it to
an AI assistant (usually Claude, sometimes Codex), and go back and forth until it reads well.

The process:

1. I pick a topic and write the full meat of it in one sitting: no structure, pure thought
   stream.
2. The AI gives it a structure.
3. I rewrite the parts I don't like.
4. We repeat steps 2 and 3 for as many rounds as it takes.
5. Whatever comes out the other end gets shipped.

The ideas and the takes are mine; the AI helps with the structure and the wording.

Do I think it's a bit slop? Sure. But I've read enough slop online to make my peace with it,
and at least this is my slop. Most of what I write about is vibecoded anyway, so it fits.
